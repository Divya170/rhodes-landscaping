Our approach

Good landscaping starts with the space you already have.

Every property has its own light, layout, and way of being used. Rhodes Landscaping begins by looking closely at yours — how you move through it, where you gather, and what you would like it to become.

From there, planting and improvements are planned to suit the property, so the finished space feels considered, comfortable, and genuinely yours.

01 / 04

Garden Design

Planning and shaping outdoor spaces as a whole — the structure of the garden, how planting is arranged, and how each area relates to the next — so the finished space feels cohesive rather than pieced together.

  • A cohesive plan for the whole space
  • Planting and structure that work together
  • A clearer picture before work begins

  • Reviewing layout, light, and existing features
  • Planning garden structure such as beds, borders, and paths
  • Developing planting arrangements
  • Outlining how the design could be carried out

02 / 04

Lawn Care

Attention to the lawn's appearance and upkeep, helping it look even, tidy, and in keeping with the rest of the property.

  • A neater, more cared-for appearance
  • Edges and surrounding areas kept tidy
  • A lawn that complements planting and paths

  • Mowing and edging
  • Tidying lawn borders and surrounding areas
  • Discussing the lawn's condition and upkeep needs

Planting

Selecting and arranging plants for texture, colour, and seasonal character — bringing greenery into the property in a way that suits its setting and style.

  • Layered texture and visual interest
  • Plants chosen with the setting in mind
  • Greenery that softens and frames the home

  • Guidance on plant selection
  • New planting beds and borders
  • Refreshing existing planting areas
